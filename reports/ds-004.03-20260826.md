## Break-even conclusion

The result agrees with `reports/ab-20260821.json`: Headroom speculation can
accept most drafts yet remains slightly negative on the short Orion vision
case (previously 79.63% and -140.690 ms; now 88.64% and -40.640 ms).
Quality is deterministic and approximately decode-neutral under strict
parity, but it performs no speculative drafting and has worse prefill here.
The isolated short text case showed a Headroom decode-rate improvement, but
support overhead prevented end-to-end break-even. Therefore the catalog is
valid and parity is preserved, but this evidence does **not** support
advertising DSpark as a general speedup.

Before every large launch, the runner recorded live DS4/oMLX/Pi processes,
DS4 locks, ports 8000/18082, exact catalog artifact handles, and memory. Each
launch started with no DS4 process/lock/artifact handle, oMLX unloaded with no
model handles, and 89-91% memory free. Runs were serialized; unrelated oMLX
and Pi processes were untouched. Final postflight found no DS4 process,
lock, port 18082 activity, or catalog handle, with 90% memory free.
