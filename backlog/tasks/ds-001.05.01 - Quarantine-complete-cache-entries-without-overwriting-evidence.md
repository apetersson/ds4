## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Review pass 1 finding. The recovery command quarantines only the artifact, leaves .ds4-meta blocking later acquisition, and uses a fixed destination that can overwrite earlier evidence.

Practical likelihood: Medium. Trigger: any checksum mismatch on a normally published cache entry; repeated corruption makes destination collision realistic. Impact: the advertised recovery cannot recover and may destroy prior quarantine evidence. Scope: Required for DS-001.05. Disposition: Include now.
