## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Determine why an image-aware build can understand that an image exists yet produce semantically wrong or hallucinated content. Verify the complete FlyCockpit tower/projector/receiver alignment before considering any ablation of vision weights; separate preprocessing, tiling, token placement, projector, receiver, and refusal effects with causal controls.
<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:END -->

## Acceptance Criteria
<!-- AC:BEGIN -->
- [ ] #1 Exact tower and projector provenance, hashes, expected receiver family, hidden width, image token ID, route tokens, tiling configuration, and training-time prompt geometry are recorded.
- [ ] #2 A trivial screenshot/OCR set is evaluated with correct image, zero embeddings, shuffled image, shuffled rows, wrong projector, and text-only controls.
- [ ] #3 Correct-image outputs are causally and semantically distinguishable from zero/shuffled controls at 257 and 769 token layouts.
- [ ] #4 Intermediate embedding norms, finite-value checks, row ordering, separator placement, receiver logits, and repeatability identify semantic drift before subjective scoring.
- [ ] #5 Vision-tower or projector ablation is not performed unless a reproducible refusal behavior remains after alignment and is localized to those weights.
- [ ] #6 If ablation is justified, the exact tensor scope, method, retained checkpoint, before/after controls, and regression thresholds are specified as new follow-up tasks.
- [ ] #7 The final evidence concludes aligned/no-ablation, receiver-only ablation sufficient, or vision-side intervention required.
<!-- AC:END -->
