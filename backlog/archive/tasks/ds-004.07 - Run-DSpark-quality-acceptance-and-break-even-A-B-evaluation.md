## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Measure whether preserved-MXFP4 DSpark provides correct and practically beneficial speculative decoding for Headroom128 and Quality128. Report target-only versus DSpark wall time, draft/accept/replay costs, quality parity, memory, and break-even behavior rather than relying on acceptance rate alone.
<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:END -->

## Acceptance Criteria
<!-- AC:BEGIN -->
- [ ] #1 A/B runs use identical receiver hash, prompts, sampler settings, seeds, context, backend, power state, and warmed/cold conditions.
- [ ] #2 Reports include drafted, accepted, rejected, replayed, and net-saved tokens plus draft, verify, replay, and total wall time.
- [ ] #3 Short visual-answer, short text, coding, reasoning, long-prefill, and tool-style workloads are represented.
- [ ] #4 Strict/exact modes meet correctness criteria and ordinary mode reports any output divergence with quality scoring.
- [ ] #5 DSpark is not advertised as faster for a workload when end-to-end median wall time is worse despite high acceptance.
- [ ] #6 Headroom128 and Quality128 results include measured peak unified memory and confidence intervals over repeated runs.
- [ ] #7 The report recommends default-off, workload-conditional, or default-on behavior from measured break-even evidence.
<!-- AC:END -->
